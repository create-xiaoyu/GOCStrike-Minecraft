# Logo generation

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. The final PNG is stored at
`src/main/resources/gocstrike-logo.png` and is used by NeoForge's mod information
panel and the README. The in-game item retains its existing inventory icon.

## Prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished square logo for the Minecraft NeoForge mod 'GOC Strike' (Chinese name 奇术打击), to be used in the mod information panel and GitHub README. Create one polished, original gaming emblem on an almost-black midnight navy square background. The visual identity comes from its giant animated arcane magic circle: a crisp upright white five-point star inscribed in two bold cyan and violet concentric sigil rings, with a luminous downward strike lance extending from the star through the bottom of the rings; a few angular voxel-like crystal facets frame the ring without clutter. Strong iconic silhouette, centered symmetrical composition, generous 10% safe margin, sharp geometric edges, restrained cyan/violet Bloom with a brilliant white core. Premium stylized game logo, clear and readable at 128 px. Beneath the emblem set the exact words 'GOC STRIKE' in strong clean condensed geometric uppercase lettering, large and well spaced, pale ice white with subtle violet accents. No other text, no tagline, no mockup, no scenery, no character, no watermark, no official SCP or other organization seal. The design must feel like a deliberate compact brand emblem, with simplified bold rune details and excellent negative space rather than a busy illustration. 1:1 aspect ratio.
```
